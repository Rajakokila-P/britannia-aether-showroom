02 / Vehicle approval & road use

These four concepts have no claimed type approval, registration, roadworthiness certification or tested performance. Their floating appearance is fictional. Ordinary road-vehicle guidance must not be interpreted as permission to operate a hovercar or aircraft.

For a real vehicle, the appropriate approval route must be established before sale or registration where required. GOV.UK distinguishes type approval from individual vehicle approval, with exemptions and different schemes depending on the vehicle. A future manufacturer would need to confirm the applicable route with the relevant authority.

Public-road use also carries separate requirements concerning the driver’s licence, insurance, registration, tax, roadworthiness and MOT where applicable. Driver-assistance proposals do not authorise driverless use or remove the driver’s responsibilities.

Official sources: GOV.UK — Vehicle approval · GOV.UK — Drivers’ and riders’ legal obligations.

03 / Consumer rights & future sales

No orders, deposits, finance applications or payments are accepted here. Saving a concept brief does not create a purchase contract.

Before any future online sale, a trader would need clear information about its identity and contact details, the goods, total price and taxes, payment, delivery, cancellation and applicable terms. Online customers must be able to review and correct an order and retain the terms.

Consumer protections cannot be removed by a website notice. Faulty, misdescribed or unfit goods can give rise to statutory remedies. A commercial warranty is additional to those rights. No warranty, guarantee or automatic replacement cover is currently offered for these concepts.

Distance sales generally carry cancellation rights, subject to the applicable rules and exceptions. Any future vehicle sales policy must assess the actual contract and goods rather than assume every vehicle order has identical cancellation rights.

Official sources: GOV.UK — Distance selling · GOV.UK — Online selling · GOV.UK — Returns and refunds.

04 / Advertising & technology claims

UK advertising must describe products accurately and be truthful and socially responsible. AI-generated imagery on this site illustrates design ideas, not production vehicles or demonstrated capabilities.

Hovering, voice and movement sensing, adaptive ride, smart glass and connected features remain proposals. No claim is made that the vehicles predict human behaviour, prevent accidents, withstand potholes or meet a particular range, power or charging specification. “British-inspired” describes the creative identity; it is not proof of UK manufacture.

Official source: GOV.UK — Marketing and advertising law.

05 / Data protection & this website

Businesses using personal data must comply with applicable data-protection requirements, protect that information and explain who is using it, for what purpose and how individuals can exercise their rights.

The showroom’s configurator runs in your browser and creates a local text download. It does not ask for your name, address or payment details, and the showroom code does not submit your selections to a sales team. No microphone, camera or movement sensing is activated by this website.

The hosting service may process access and sign-in information. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts. This description concerns the showroom’s own functions and is not a claim that the hosting platform processes no data. Any future enquiry forms, analytics, marketing or vehicle sensing would require a suitable privacy notice and assessment of the applicable consent and data-protection requirements.

Official source: GOV.UK — Data protection and your business.
